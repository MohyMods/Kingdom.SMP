# Concise manual acceptance

Use a backed-up/disposable world and matching client/server JARs. These are manual follow-up checks, not claims of executed graphical testing.

1. Start a fresh world; owner claims Hut plan, previews/rotates/submits a permitted site, builds with original Aldric. Interact with crafting/workstation furniture; confirm Aldric remains available.
2. Two players contribute partial resources, verify permanent consumption/shared progress/live normalized leaderboard. Finish Tier 1; verify unique winner/Tier 2 Town Hall plan, progressive original-Aldric construction and sole Steward.
3. Complete displayed Tier 2 requirements, road inspection and paid Bank; progress naturally to Tier 6. Check locked future destinations and Coming Soon controls; test both FTB and OPAC denials.
4. Earn each selected profession's 24 quests and Master rank. Native tests cover full quantities with supplied raw inputs/time acceleration; calibrate actual community duration and economy balance during play.
5. At each Master counter, buy its signature, check quoted 50%-compounded repeat price, native private mail, non-Master/inactive denial and legitimate trading. Try each signature using the player guide, including shortage/stale inventory/claim refusal.
6. Check Royal Gilded menus at small/large GUI scales: no clipped names, review text, buttons, tooltips or cost fields. Inspect all new item and workstation models.
7. Buy/approve/construct Aerodrome at earned Tier 6; verify Tier 6 without a completed building still denies aircraft, native board/flight/land/share/repair/pack/deploy operate safely. Aircraft registers to buyer with explicit shared access.
8. Exercise banking, all auction modes, mail, contracts and receipts with two clients. Restart with completed and interrupted operations; confirm no duplicated money, gear, buildings or NPCs.
9. Load a copied legacy Lodge world: original Lodge/Mira, player progress, entitlements, claims and construction remain unchanged.
10. Validate the owner's full 277-mod pack separately. The automated compatibility profile contains 29 pinned artifacts and cannot certify every additional mod.
