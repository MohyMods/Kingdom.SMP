# Playing the Alpha

On a dedicated server, OP4 first reviews `/kingdom admin owner <player>` and pins that initial owner with `/kingdom admin owner <player> confirm`. This cannot reassign an existing owner. The owner presses **K → Construction** to claim the free Builder's Hut plan. Use its hologram to choose and rotate a safe site, submit it for required site approval, and confirm through Royal Gilded controls. Aldric constructs approved buildings progressively; keep the chunks loaded normally. Workstation interactions must not remove his Hut assignment.

Donate real Tier 1 resources at the kingdom counter. The shared leaderboard records normalized contributions. Finishing Tier 1 reaches Tier 2; the winner receives the unique Town Hall entitlement. Recover a lost unredeemed plan through the existing entitlement interface rather than duplicating it. The Town Hall's Steward provides overview, contributions and milestones. Follow the displayed objectives, approved building/road inspections and paid Bank path through Tier 6. The Aerodrome requires both Tier 6 and its completed building; reaching Tier 6 alone never grants aircraft access.

At each paid profession building, enroll, inspect the quest board and perform its actual objectives. Maximum three active professions; inactive progress remains saved. Switching costs 15,000 coins. Each selected profession has 8 Apprentice, 8 Journeyman and 8 Master quests. Some tasks require a real operator's on-site inspection or a consenting current customer's service job. Inventory alone cannot substitute for native crafting, mining or source history. Older unknown sources are not retroactively certified. Library future-tier requirements use the documented Tier 6 Alpha variants.

Money can be earned through implemented NPC buying, contracts and available community services. Use Bank deposits/withdrawals, private coin/item mail and auction escrow through their actual counters. Regular auction tax is 5%; Master equipment tax is 10%. Tax leaves circulation. Read every review before confirming.

## Master equipment

Only the corresponding **active Master** can buy at their staffed, maintained profession building. Purchases arrive as private item mail. They may subsequently be traded and used by anyone. Each player's repeat price for each item compounds by 50%, rounded up. The five new rewards default to 50,000 coins; existing Farmer prices are unchanged.

- **Farmer — Fortune X Hoe:** existing crop reward, unchanged.
- **Blacksmith — Extraction Anvil:** place and use the workstation. Put the source in hotbar slot 1; keep blank books and lapis in the main inventory. Select one enchantment in the Royal Gilded review. Default cost: 8 XP levels, 4 lapis and one blank book. Curses and protected signature items cannot be extracted. Other enchantments and item data remain intact.
- **Builder — Architect's Wand:** hold a supported plain full-cube building material in the offhand and real supplies in the main inventory. Click two corners to preview. Sneak-right-click air cycles line, wall, floor and matching replacement. Look north/south versus east/west to rotate walls. Confirm in the Royal Gilded screen. Defaults: at most 128 blocks, each axis at most 16 blocks. The offhand is a selector; main supplies pay the operation. Replacement permanently replaces reviewed matching ordinary blocks without refunds. Containers, ore, machinery and valuable terrain are excluded.
- **Lumberjack — Worldtree Axe:** unbreakable, Efficiency VI. Mine an eligible tracked tree to queue bounded native felling; sneak to mine a single block. Carry appropriate saplings for automatic replanting. Unknown old trees are conservatively mined one block at a time. Newly grown compatible trees acquire native growth provenance. Player construction timber never becomes a tree merely because leaves are nearby. Default cap: 256 logs.
- **Miner — Prospector's Pickaxe:** unbreakable, Efficiency VI, Fortune VI. Mine eligible connected ore with normal harvest rules, up to 64 blocks per operation. Sneak for one block. Right-click surveys permitted loaded terrain within radius 6; default cooldown 10 seconds. Messages name ore types and offsets. It cannot scan other dimensions or load terrain.
- **Librarian — Enchanter's Codex:** place and use the workstation; source in hotbar slot 1, one enchanted donor book in slot 2, lapis in the main inventory. Review compatible improvements and discounted XP. Equal native-maximum enchantments can advance one level beyond their native maximum, bounded by config. Defaults: maximum 80 XP levels per service, 4 lapis, advanced level cap 10. Real books and XP are consumed. Incompatible combinations, free enchantments and signature modification are refused.

Close a review to cancel. Reviews expire and recheck inventory, XP, permissions and site. Never copy or edit item data to recover a purchase. Keep pending economy/world/player saves together if an interrupted operation requires administrator recovery.
