# Alpha Master equipment — unresolved behavior review

Approved identities are Extraction Anvil (Blacksmith), Architect's Wand (Builder), Worldtree Axe (Lumberjack), Prospector's Pickaxe (Miner), and a separately undefined Librarian signature. The reviewed directives name the first four but do not supply exact abilities. Farmer's existing Fortune X Hoe is implemented and stays unchanged. Purchasing requires the corresponding active Master at its maintained profession building, with private recoverable delivery and personal/item 50% compounded repeat pricing. Trading/use by other players remains allowed. Numerical prices, ranges and work budgets can be configured under existing owner authority.

The existing focused question about Prospector ability and Librarian reward remains unanswered; do not duplicate it or invent either item. The three proposals below are faithful interpretations of the named concepts, **PROPOSED behavior, not previously approved or implemented**. They need one design clarification before activation. This is not a claim that Master equipment is complete.

| Signature | Proposed actual ability | Safety and material behavior |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Worldtree Axe | Fell the remaining logs of the same genuinely tracked native tree after a successful normal player cut. Sneaking performs ordinary single-block chopping. | Native successful breaks, tool wear and drops; current independent claims/reservations checked per block; bounded per tick; no permanent chunk loading. Placed construction timber and unknown old trees never become guessed natural provenance. Interrupt safely on player/tool/dimension/claim changes. |
| Architect's Wand | Preview and place a bounded line or plane of plain building blocks selected from the player's inventory; confirm the server-derived plan. | Consume actual blocks only for successful work, preserve full placement/support/collision/world/reservation/claim checks, never replace player property or generate free materials. Recovery must not duplicate material or repeat a completed placement. |
| Extraction Anvil | Transfer a selected ordinary enchantment from current inventory equipment onto a real blank book while keeping the remaining source equipment. | Remove the transferred enchantment from the source in the same transaction; charge actual book/XP; forbid protected signature and unvalidated mod components. Canonical native inventory recovery, explicit review and once-only confirmation. No duplicated enchantment or output. |

Native acceptance must include earned Master purchase/use, wrong/inactive role denial, price escalation/replay/writer failure/native save/restart, claim revocation, genuine material and durability costs, physical outputs and non-owner use through a legitimate trade. No placeholder item or purchase-only prototype counts as delivered equipment.
