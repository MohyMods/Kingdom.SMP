# Profession and community services

Use an empty hand to speak with a named resident. Public claims need the explicit settings in PUBLIC_NPC_CLAIM_SETUP.md; the mod never grants build or attack permission to open dialogue.

At the Farmer’s House, open Roles and review Farmer enrollment. The first three professions learned are free; later changes/reactivation cost 15,000 coins and keep inactive XP/quests. Inactive professions grant no progression credit, bonuses or equipment purchase rights. Progress and ready objectives appear below the roster; future bands stay locked. Complete eight Apprentice objectives for Journeyman, then eight Journeyman objectives for Master trials, then all eight Master trials for Master. Farmer objectives are approved D-034. Other profession quest content remains unavailable.

Plant crops yourself and harvest at maturity. Previously constructed or copied mature crops grant no harvest credit until their real seeded lifecycle is recorded. Tilling counts distinct cells; breeding counts unique attributed offspring. Replant trials require your seed → mature harvest → real seed replant cycle. Real crafting alone does not bypass consumed-food proof. Food deliveries are reviewed at Rowan’s counter and consumed permanently; exact emptied buckets/bowls return to inventory. Farm inspections require a real administrator at the site and a confirmed recorded approval. Daily/weekly contract objectives count distinct periods completed during their eligible objective band.

Contracts reset daily at 00:00 UTC or weekly Monday at 00:00 UTC. Matching active professions deliver at their own residents. Exact items are consumed after review; payment goes to private coin mail. Shared reward pools are finite and persisted. Proposed later profession/mod integrations are not enabled merely because an item exists.

At Tier 3, the Architect offers one Royal Bounty Hall Blueprint (1,500-coin default). The first buyer chooses its location using the authenticated plan/hologram. Aldric builds the original furnished hall progressively, then its permanent Bounty Master Garrick Thornfield arrives. His food board accepts partial reviewed supplies and pays coins through private mail. Master Farmer community proof requires a completed project with at least 10% of its goal personally supplied during Master trials. Early contributions still earn money but cannot retrospectively become Master proofs. Hunting contracts are not implemented yet.

Farmer equipment is purchased at the actual Farmer after earning the corresponding active rank. Fieldwarden’s Hoe: Journeyman, 5,000-coin default, Fortune III and 5×5 crop work. Fortune X Hoe: Master, 25,000-coin base default, 9×9. Master repeats cost ceiling(base×1.5^previous successful purchases), separately for each player/item. Purchases deliver through private Mailbox; mastery grants the right to buy, not a free item. These numerical defaults are configurable server-side. Trading and use by others are allowed.

Right-click mature vanilla wheat/carrots/potatoes/beetroot with a supplied-seed hoe. Hold seeds/planting produce in main inventory; each replant consumes a real item and durability. Sneak for one crop. Harvesting uses normal protected break/place interactions and bounded server work, stops when the held tool changes, and loads no chunks. A denied block or missing seeds does not become a free harvest. Unknown modded crops are not certified. Ordinary auction tax stays 5%; registered Master gear 10%, removed permanently. Buy orders accept plain vanilla items or pristine registered Farmer equipment; damaged/custom variants require supported fixed/timed listings.

Actual operator tools: /kingdom admin professions inspect <player>; /kingdom admin contracts inspect <player>; /kingdom admin farmer inspect <player> <quest-id> <x y z> <reason>, then click its 30-second exact confirmation. Inspection checks active band, loaded site, target permissions, physical farm minimums and administrator proximity again before a permanent actor-attributed completion. It edits no claims or terrain. The general 1.0 admin dashboard, all 12 professions, full 24-objective player acceptance and the complete modpack remain unfinished.
