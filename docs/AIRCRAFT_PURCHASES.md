# Buyer-owned aircraft and shared access

D050 ownership approval is implemented at Corin Windlass's actual Aerodrome counter. Supplies lists the seven registered Immersive Aircraft1.7.0 airframes; purchase review debits the canonical purse and creates buyer registration and private native-item delivery together. Mailbox uses the existing saved inventory receipt. Aircraft are not first-buyer kingdom building rewards.

All purchases require Tier6, the completed/intact Aerodrome, the actual nearby registered Corin, clear sight, ordinary living Survival/Adventure and current FTB/OPAC interaction permission. Review expires after30seconds and is invalidated by journal revision changes. All seven retain the existing common Tier6 gate; no subtype schedule was invented. Numerical prices in the world `serverconfig/kingdomsmp-aircraft.json` are configurable starting defaults, not finalized multiplayer balance: Bamboo Hopper8,000, Quadrocopter12,000, Gyrodyne16,000, Biplane20,000, Airship30,000, Cargo Airship45,000, Warship60,000. Fuel/upgrades are separate.

The buyer can review granting access to an online player or revoking existing crew, including offline crew. Ownership never transfers. Shared access covers placement, boarding, controls and cargo; both independent claim policies and kingdom gates still apply. Revoke invalidates current native menu/control rights immediately; the existing periodic safe-ground policy handles a displaced rider. Read-only `/kingdom admin aircraft inspect` is OP2 and shows canonical registration, buyer, phase, generation and crew.

## Authority and recovery

Registration is a projection of the authenticated existing economy journal, including closed archives. No second balance/registration file or client-supplied price grants authority. Physical native-item markers contain journal identity/registration/generation only. Deployment restores canonical native components, validates the normal full loaded footprint/world border/height/reservations/claims/collision, and consumes one item using the native saved-player receipt protocol. Failed preflight checks consume nothing.

An exact committed consumption creates STARTING authority with a fixed native UUID/origin. The corresponding body is inert until durably saved before ACTIVE commits. Recovery waits for the original actor, current permissions and already-loaded footprint; it never permanently loads chunks. ACTIVE aircraft are never respawned merely because an entity is absent/unloaded. Existing unregistered aircraft keep their previous native behavior and gates.

When native packing/destruction actually produces a remaining airframe under native drop settings, it goes to the buyer's private mail with a new generation, rather than an unauthenticated world-item drop. Native cargo/drop behavior remains native; this is not an insurance policy or a free replacement after every loss. Old physical copies cannot deploy, and superseded old saved bodies are discarded without creating cargo/item drops. Native crafting recipes consuming purchased airframes would erase registration: the Ingredient boundary refuses marked airframes while preserving unregistered crafting. Equipment still fits through native aircraft inventory. Other mods' custom recipe/transform routes require full-pack review.

Storage/authority errors fail closed. Back up the whole matching world/economy archive/player set. Do not restore an individual old player file, delete registration records, downgrade to a JAR unable to read new journal kinds or issue replacement active aircraft by guessing whether their chunks contain them. Administrative lost-active-frame adjudication remains unimplemented. D053 adds reviewed buyer cancellation of STARTING only, returning canonical private mail/new generation and invalidating the pending body and copies; ACTIVE records can never use this recovery path.
