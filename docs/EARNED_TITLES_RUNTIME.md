# Earned cosmetic titles

`/kingdom title` opens the Royal Gilded selector without requiring a trade counter. The exchange also has a Titles tab. Players review and select one earned title, or clear it. Selection is free and self-only. This changes chat and the player list; it grants no operator, property, inventory, construction-management or profession benefits. Existing scoreboard team formatting and untitled player-list behavior are preserved.

Founder labels come from the existing catalogue and canonical first successful purchase, preserving the already-announced founder identity. Profession choices include every genuinely earned rank in the canonical roster, including inactive tracks; inactive bonuses stay disabled. Unpurchased buildings, other buyers' titles, unearned Master ranks and undefined achievement titles are never granted. This is the founder/profession selector slice, not completion of all achievement titles or unapproved profession content.

The bounded strict schema1 `data/kingdomsmp_titles.dat` and `.created` identity pair bind preferences to the economy/world. Preferences cannot grant achievements: current server records are rechecked for review, confirmation and display. Missing/foreign/malformed files or uncertain writes pause preferences locally; existing economy and gameplay remain available. Ordinary login refreshes cosmetic name caches only after vanilla finishes registering the online player. Network protocol14 requires matching client/server for the added tab9; world progression, construction and economy save schemas are unchanged.
