# Alchemist native brewing

Approved, implemented routes: all eight Apprentice objectives; Journeyman j01extended fire resistance and j02strong healing. Other later objectives remain unavailable until their own validated producers exist. Farmer24is unchanged.

Use the original Alchemist laboratory/Nessa. An operator inspects the accessible charged brewing stand, actual water cauldron, lighting and storage for a01. Place blaze powder in the fuel slot and let the native stand charge. Filling/emptying a vanilla cauldron preserves building integrity; no blocks or contents are replaced by Kingdom SMP.

Load water/awkward potion bottles first. Insert the correct reagent **last** into an idle permitted stand. For strength, place blaze powder in the ingredient slot; vanilla shift-click normally routes it to fuel. Leave the inputs untouched until the native cycle finishes. Stand ownership/merely opening a GUI does not assign credit: actual player inventory/cursor reagent debit does. Its original dimension and block position are pinned; copied or moved pending records cannot authenticate another stand. A successful native fuel charge and reagent-consuming transformation are required.

Collect your own completed batch. Only successful extraction at the original live stand, with current claims, active profession/band, tier and prerequisites, authenticates each exact output. Other players may use ordinary potions, but cannot claim your profession proof. At Nessa, review and confirm the potion delivery through the normal Royal Gilded profession menu. Default12physical potions and their distinct output rights are consumed together for100Apprentice or200Journeyman XPonce. Configurable1–36goals are in world/serverconfig/kingdomsmp-brewing-quests.json; existing files stay unchanged.

A pending attributed batch persists with the real stand and its native brew timer/fuel. It can finish while the operator is offline; original operator/current permissions are rechecked at collection. Input changes invalidate unfinished attribution. Broken/moved/replaced stands, modified ingredients and unrelated or fabricated item tags do not qualify. Corrupt optional provenance is rejected without interrupting vanilla inventory movement or brewing. Canonical output/spent identities remain the authority for delivery and XP, with existing native receipt/recovery.

Journeyman j01uses native fire-resistance +redstone →long fire resistance; j02uses native healing +glowstone →strong healing. Both need Tier6and the complete earned Apprentice band. Neither invents a dependency on the other.
