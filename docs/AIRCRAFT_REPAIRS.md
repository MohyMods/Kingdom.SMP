# Corin's workshop repairs

D054 adds an actual paid convenience service for buyer-registered aircraft. Native food-based hand repairs remain available unchanged. Park the original unoccupied aircraft on the ground, stop its engine and visit Corin with the aircraft within eight blocks. The completed/intact Aerodrome, Tier6, living Survival/Adventure counter access and independent current FTB/OPAC permissions are required. Legacy unregistered aircraft retain native hand repairs; they are not silently assigned an owner or converted.

The Royal counter shows current health and a reviewed exact price. World `serverconfig/kingdomsmp-aircraft-repairs.json` sets the numerical `fullRepairRate` default2,000coins. Charge is ceil((1−health)×rate), using deterministic decimal arithmetic on normalized native health; full/dead/nonfinite health cannot start a paid repair. This is a configurable numerical default, not finalized multiplayer balance or a new profession gate. Only the buyer finances/manages this service; existing shared crew hand repairs/use remain unchanged.

Payment reserves coins in the existing canonical journal. The actual native health change and service UUID are world-saved before the journal converts the reservation into a completed shop charge. A pending airframe cannot be moved, damaged through native damage, controlled, boarded or accessed through its player cargo menu. Recovery waits for the original loaded aircraft and valid workshop access before applying undelivered work. An already saved matching receipt settles without healing again, even if health subsequently changed. A known loaded airframe with no delivered receipt can return exactly the original reserved coins through a reviewed cancellation; absent/unloaded airframes never authorize guessed refunds. A replay cannot charge, heal or refund twice.

Receipt authority binds the exact registered native type/UUID, journal identity and airframe generation. Last delivered service UUID is reconstructed from authenticated history/archives. Unknown/malformed service receipts or mismatched world/journal saves fail closed. A legitimate next-generation packing resets its old receipt expectation; pending paid repairs must be resolved before packing. No replacement active aircraft, ownership transfer, resource grant, forced chunk ticket or new native repair configuration is introduced.

`/kingdom admin aircraft inspect` remains OP2/read-only and now includes pending service IDs/actors, reserved amounts and total held repair coins (bounded output). Storage failures quarantine financial operations. Restore the complete matching world/entity/player/journal/seal/archive backup; do not independently roll back one player or entity file or delete a pending service.
